Privacy Policy

Motion Cam (Android) · Effective date: November 16, 2026

In short
  • Videos and photos you record stay on your phone. We have no server and never upload them.
  • You don't need an account, and we don't collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID.

1Who we are

Motion Cam ("the app") is developed and operated by J.Biglion ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

2Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and the app never sends it anywhere.

  • Recordings: videos and photos taken when motion is detected. They are saved in the app's own folder and, only when you tap "Save to gallery", copied to your phone's gallery.
  • Detection history: the time of each detection, motion level, and the list of files that belong to it.
  • Settings: sensitivity, detection area, video options, camera, storage limit, display mode, language, and similar preferences.
  • Camera frames used for detection: the camera image is analysed in memory to measure motion. Frames that aren't recorded are discarded immediately.

The app does not add location, faces, or any other personal tags to recordings, and does not use facial recognition.

3Permissions and why we ask

PermissionPurpose

You can deny or revoke any permission in your phone's settings. Without the camera permission the app can't monitor.

4Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • The advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic information

This information is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The ads never have access to your recordings.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and similar regions): the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings > About > "Ad privacy options".
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Some optional features (for example "Save to gallery" or certain settings) offer a short rewarded ad. Watching it is voluntary, and nothing about your recordings is shared with the ad.

5Information we collect

We, the developer, do not run a server and do not collect, sell, or share personal information. Apart from the advertising SDK described above, the app contains no analytics or tracking tools.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

6How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • Recordings and history stay on your phone until you delete them in "Detection history", or until the storage limit you set removes the oldest files automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its folder, recordings, history, and settings. Files you saved to the gallery remain there until you delete them yourself.
  • Data handled by Google for advertising is kept under Google's policies.

7Security

Recordings are stored in the app's private folder, which other apps can't read without your action. Because nothing is uploaded, keeping your phone protected with a screen lock is the best way to keep recordings safe.

8Using the camera responsibly

Motion Cam is meant for watching your own space, such as your home, pets, or shop. Recording people without their knowledge may be illegal where you live. You are responsible for following local laws on recording, consent, and privacy, and for telling people when a camera is in use where required.

9Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. Because we don't hold your recordings or account data, you can manage them directly in the app. For questions about ad data, use the options above or contact Google. You can also contact us at the address below.

11Changes to this policy

If the app's features or the information it handles change, we'll update this policy and the effective date above.
